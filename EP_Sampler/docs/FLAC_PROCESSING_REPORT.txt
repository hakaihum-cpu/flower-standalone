Findings

- Therefore there are 8 unique PCM sources, not 24 distinct performances.
- Note timing matches the capture manifest: first Note On at 2.000 s, then 9.000 s slots.

- The final 100 ms is digital silence for every velocity layer, so the C8 tail is not audibly truncated at EOF.

Bank strategy
- EPBANK1 remains logically 88 keys x 8 velocities x 3 RR x 2 parts = 4,224 index entries.
- Identical RR entries share the same PCM offsets instead of storing the same PCM three times.
- PCM is preserved at 48 kHz / stereo / 24-bit.
- No normalization, limiting, compression, or level modification is applied.
