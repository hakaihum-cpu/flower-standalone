After rendering, place the 24 files in one folder:

EP_V016_RR1.wav ... EP_V127_RR3.wav

Do not put individual sliced notes here. The bank builder uses tools/capture_manifest.csv.

Build command example:
python tools/build_bank.py bank_input -o epbank.bin --target-bits 24

Then start the app, tap the small BANK indicator at the top-left, and select epbank.bin.
The app copies it into its private storage and memory-maps it.
